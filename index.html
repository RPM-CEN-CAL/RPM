Visitor & Buyer Assistance Guide

Step-by-step directions for equipment buyers and prospective seller accounts.

Step 1: Equipment Search & Valuation Free Visitors

Browse active machinery listings directly from the Home page. Use the integrated Valuation Calculator to compute market estimates based on age, hours, and condition prior to making seller offers.

Step 2: Selecting a Membership Tier Sellers

Select your desired active listing volume ($5 for 1 listing, $10 for 5, $20 for 15, or $50 for unlimited). Click "Select Tier" to highlight your selection.

Step 3: Account Creation & Payment Account Setup

Enter your business email and a secure password in the Account Setup section. Click proceed to redirect directly to Square Payment Gateway.

Step 4: Existing Sellers Login Client Portal

If you already possess an active paid seller subscription, click "Client Portal" in the top header menu to sign into your seller dashboard.
